Research & Publications

Towards Malicious Address Identification in Bitcoin

IEEE 2021 International Conference on Blockchain, Melbourne, Australia · pp. 425–432

The temporal aspect of blockchain transactions enables us to study address behavior and detect illicit activity. We study the Bitcoin transaction network using temporal features such as burst, attractiveness, and inter-event time, along with graph-based properties to validate ML-based approaches for malicious address detection. ...

After applying change-address clustering in Bitcoin, existing temporal features can be extracted and ML approaches applied. A comparative analysis shows address behavior in Ethereum and Bitcoin is similar with respect to in-degree, out-degree, and inter-event time. We identify 3 suspects showing malicious behavior across different temporal granularities not previously marked as malicious in Bitcoin.

Under guidance of Prof. Sandeep K. Shukla & Dr. Rachit Agarwal, IIT Kanpur.
